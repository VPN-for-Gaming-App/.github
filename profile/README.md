## 🚀 Overview
VPN for Gaming for Windows is a gaming-focused VPN that aims to make online play smoother and safer. By routing your connection through optimized, low-latency servers, it can help reduce ping on busy routes, shield your real IP from DDoS attacks, and let you connect to game servers in other regions. It keeps a clean, gamer-friendly interface so you can pick a server and get back to playing. For anyone chasing steadier matches and fewer interruptions, this VPN for Gaming keeps the essentials front and center on your PC.

## ✨ Features
- **Low-latency routing** — Optimized paths that can help reduce ping on congested routes.
- **DDoS protection** — Hide your real IP to guard against targeted attacks.
- **Region access** — Connect to game servers and stores in other regions.
- **No bandwidth throttling** — A VPN tunnel that avoids ISP-based slowdowns on some routes.
- **Kill switch** — Keep your IP protected if the connection drops.

## ❓ FAQ
**What does a VPN do for gaming?**
A gaming VPN routes your traffic through its own servers, which can help reduce ping on some routes, hide your IP from DDoS attacks, and let you reach game servers in other regions.

**Which VPN protocol is best for gaming?**
A lightweight, modern protocol usually gives the best balance of speed and stability for gaming. The app selects a fast protocol by default and lets you choose one that suits your connection.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
It runs on modern 64-bit Windows, including Windows 10 and Windows 11. Installing the latest stable release is recommended.
